At exactly 2:17 AM, Alex received a phone notification.

It was a photograph of his bedroom.

He was sleeping in the picture.

Confused, he checked his phone gallery. The photograph had been taken just seconds ago.

Then another notification arrived.

It was a message from an unknown number.

"Don't turn around."

Alex froze.

Another message appeared.

"Because I'm already behind you."

His bedroom suddenly went silent.

Then his phone rang.

The caller ID displayed his own name.
